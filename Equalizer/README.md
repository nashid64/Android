# Equalizer
As i personally like to tune audio for different kinda speakers.. I made an equalizer for myself and if anyone interested.
A multi-band audio equalizer for Android, designed for audiophiles. Boost or cut individual frequency bands to shape how your phone sounds.

The interface is inspired by the Samsung equalizer and built specifically for Samsung devices running **One UI**.

## Overview

Equalizer is tuned for **built-in phone speakers only**. It is not optimized for headphones or external speakers, so results on those outputs may not be ideal. The goal is to let you reshape the sound of the phone's own speakers to your taste.

## Features

- Adjust multiple frequency bands to boost or cut specific parts of the sound
- Theme and layout inspired by the Samsung One UI equalizer
- Optimized for the built-in speakers of Samsung phones

## Compatibility

- **Designed for:** Samsung devices running One UI
- **Optimized for:** Built-in phone speakers
- **Not optimized for:** Headphones and external speakers
